
### Evolutionary analysis of gene ages across TADs associates chromatin topology with whole-genome duplications
    James, Caelinn et al.
    Cell Reports, Volume 43, Issue 4, 113895

? investigated whether protein-coding genes that share a TAD had also originated around the same evolutionary time (and thus share the same evolutionary age)

! gene age is non-randomly distributed in the human and mouse genomes
! genes within the same TAD tend to have similar gene ages
! but; TADs with more inter-TAD interactions (less insulated) have younger genes
! genes born before whole-genome duplications are not close to young genes, but when they are, the young genes are essential

- genes with same ancestry colocalised in same tad?
! Gene age co-localization is stronger in TADs than in fixed-size partitions of the human genome (aka not just bc they are close together)

### The role of imprinted genes in humans
    Miho Ishida, Gudrun E. Moore
    Molecular Aspects of Medicine, Volume 34, Issue 4, July–August 2013

! review on imprinted genes
- Imprinted gene regions studied so far predominantly show differences in DNA methylation -> differently methylated region (DMR, germline and somatic)
- tend to be in clusters, often under control of cis acting loci, termed ICRs (imprinting control regions)
- common features: CpG sites, allelic histone mods, tandem repeat sequence, CTCF and YY1 sites, ncRNA
